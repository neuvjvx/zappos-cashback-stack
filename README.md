## Free returns, but not for 365 days

Search "Zappos return policy" and you'll still find the 365-day figure repeated on cashback and coupon sites. Zappos' own shipping and returns page, last updated in July, says something different [13]:

- Returns scanned by the carrier within **60 days** of purchase are refundable to your original payment method or as store credit.
- After 60 days, items may be returned **as store credit up to one year** from the purchase date.
- Guest checkout orders are limited to the 60-day window and refunds to the original payment method only.
- Final Sale items aren't returnable.
- Exchanges work by moving the funds from your original order onto an e-Gift Card so the replacement ships before your return arrives — but the original item has to be back within 14 days, or the card on file gets charged.

Free return shipping still applies, and free standard shipping is still available on every US order. The long return window is what changed, and the third-party pages quoting a year of refunds to your card are describing a policy that no longer matches the source.
